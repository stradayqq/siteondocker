The Tale of the DevOps Knight

Once upon a time, there was a young knight named Jun. His armor was made of HTML and nginx, and his sword was forged from bash scripts that hadn't quite been debugged yet.

He set off to find a castle where he could serve — remotely, without relocating, without an office, just with a laptop and stable internet.

On his journey, he encountered dragons — CI/CD pipelines, that crashed at the most the wrong moment, and the Kubernetes quagmires that even the seniors got bogged down in.

But Jun did not retreat. Every night he studied a new spell — today Docker Compose, tomorrow Ansible, the day after — the mysteries of Grafana and Loki.

And so one day, at the gates of a castle, the gatekeeper asked him: «What can you do, knight?» Jun looked up and replied: «I can learn faster than the production breaks».

The gates opened. The story continues — now in production.
